LogMyMiles

Your running history, your way.

LogMyMiles is a running journal that brings your workouts, training history, and personal reflections together in one place.

What LogMyMiles Helps With

  • Import workouts from Apple Health, Garmin, and COROS
  • Record and organize runs, routes, and training details
  • Track running shoes and other gear
  • Keep a personal running journal with notes and photos
  • Explore training trends and compare workouts
  • Create reports and shareable running images
  • Back up and restore your running history
  • Keep your running data stored locally on your device

LogMyMiles is designed for runners who want more than a workout summary — a lasting record of their training.

Privacy

LogMyMiles does not require an account. Your workouts, routes, journal entries, and photographs are stored locally on your device. The app does not use advertising or tracking.
