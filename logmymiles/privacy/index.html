Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to LogMyMiles, developed by Timber Ridge Software.

Summary

LogMyMiles is designed to keep your running information on your device.

  • LogMyMiles does not require an account.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your workouts, routes, journal entries, photographs, or health information.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Information leaves the app only when you request a service that requires it, such as displaying an Apple map, completing an App Store purchase, saving an image to Photos, or sharing or exporting a file.

Information LogMyMiles Processes

Depending on the features you use, LogMyMiles may process the following information on your device:

  • Running-workout dates, times, distances, durations, pace, and location names
  • Heart rate, cadence, steps, active energy, effort, elevation, splits, laps, and related training measurements
  • GPS route coordinates, timestamps, and altitude information
  • Workout classifications, surfaces, tags, gear, favorites, perceived effort, and feel
  • Notes and Journal entries
  • Photographs you select or capture for Journal entries or shared images
  • Age, custom maximum heart rate, threshold pace, unit preferences, and other app settings
  • Source identifiers and provenance needed to import workouts, identify recordings, prevent duplicates, and reconcile recordings from different devices or services
  • Training blocks, comparisons, reports, and generated images
  • The status of a LogMyMiles Pro purchase

This information is used to provide the features you request, including maintaining your running history, calculating statistics, displaying charts, identifying duplicate recordings, generating reports, and creating shareable images.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, LogMyMiles may request permission to read:

  • Running workouts
  • Workout routes
  • Heart rate
  • Walking and running distances
  • Active energy
  • Workout effort scores
  • Step count

LogMyMiles does not write information to Apple Health.

Health access is optional. You may review or revoke permission in the Health app or iOS Settings. Revoking access stops future access but does not automatically delete information you already imported into LogMyMiles. You can delete imported runs individually or use LogMyMiles’ data-deletion controls.

LogMyMiles uses Health information only to provide health and fitness features. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we do not sell it.

Imported Workout Files

You may import workouts from FIT files, Garmin archives, COROS archives, the Files app, or compatible document providers.

These files are decoded on your device. LogMyMiles may temporarily stage an incoming file in protected app storage while it is reviewed or imported. Staged files are removed when the import finishes, is cancelled, or fails, subject to normal operating-system file behavior.

The original file remains under the control of the app or service from which you selected it.

Routes and Apple Maps

LogMyMiles may store GPS route information imported with a workout.

When you request a map-based route image or report, LogMyMiles uses Apple MapKit. Apple may receive the geographic region needed to provide map imagery and processes that information under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

LogMyMiles does not continuously track your location and does not operate a location server.

Route privacy controls used for shared images affect only the generated image. They do not alter the original stored route.

Photographs and Images

You may select photographs from the system photo picker, capture Journal photographs with the camera, save generated images to Photos, or share generated images.

Selected Journal photographs are processed and re-encoded before storage. This generally removes metadata from the original file, including embedded camera or location metadata. Generated share images do not intentionally include original-photo metadata.

LogMyMiles requests add-only Photos permission when you choose Save to Photos. Selecting a photo through the system picker does not require LogMyMiles to receive unrestricted access to your photo library.

Local Storage and Security

LogMyMiles stores its working data in the app’s local container using Apple platform storage and data-protection facilities. Journal photographs and certain temporary files receive iOS file-protection attributes.

LogMyMiles does not use CloudKit to synchronize your database and does not upload your running history to a Timber Ridge server.

No storage method can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining your device passcode, Apple Account security, and the security of files you export.

Backups, Reports, and Sharing

You may create LogMyMiles backups, PDFs, reports, and social images, and share them through the iOS share sheet.

A LogMyMiles backup may contain sensitive information, including health measurements, GPS routes, workout history, Journal text, photographs, tags, gear, and settings.

LogMyMiles backup archives include integrity checks but **are not encrypted by LogMyMiles**. If you save a backup or report to a storage provider or another app, email, messaging, or another app, that destination’s privacy and security practices apply. Timber Ridge does not control files after you export them.

Review the contents and destination before sharing any workout, route, Journal, photograph, report, image, or backup.

Purchases

LogMyMiles Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment and Apple Account information. LogMyMiles receives only the product and verified entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features, restore purchases, and respond to refunds or revocations.

Timber Ridge does not receive your payment-card details through the app.

Third-Party Services and Software

LogMyMiles uses Apple platform services where needed:

  • HealthKit for authorized health and workout access
  • MapKit for map imagery
  • Photos and the system photo picker
  • Files and document providers
  • StoreKit for purchases
  • The native iOS share sheet

Apple processes information through these services under Apple’s privacy policy and applicable service terms.

LogMyMiles also includes software libraries for local FIT-file decoding and ZIP archive processing. These libraries are used on-device and are not used by LogMyMiles as analytics or data-collection services.

Information Timber Ridge Receives

The current version of LogMyMiles does not send your workout history, routes, Health information, Journal entries, photographs, settings, or usage activity to Timber Ridge.

If you voluntarily contact Timber Ridge for support, we will receive the information you choose to provide. Do not send sensitive health, location, or Journal information unless it is necessary for your request. Support correspondence will be retained only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain service records, protect our rights, and meet legal obligations.

Tracking and Advertising

LogMyMiles does not contain advertising and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. It does not use the advertising identifier or request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Retention and Deletion

Information stored in LogMyMiles remains on your device until you edit or delete it, reset app data, or uninstall the app, subject to Apple’s device-backup and restoration behavior.

Deleting information from LogMyMiles does not delete:

  • The original workout or health record from Apple Health
  • Photographs previously saved to Photos
  • Backups, reports, or images exported to another location
  • Copies shared with another person or service
  • Purchase records maintained by Apple

To remove those copies, you must delete them separately from their respective services or locations.

Using Delete All Data in LogMyMiles removes your locally stored running history, Journal entries and photographs, personal settings, import exclusions, and app-managed caches. It does not revoke Apple Health permissions or remove your LogMyMiles Pro purchase.

Uninstalling the app removes its local data in accordance with iOS behavior, subject to Apple's device-backup and restoration processes.

Children’s Privacy

LogMyMiles is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly operate an account service or developer backend that collects children’s personal information.
