CareBrief

Create printable care summaries for family members, caregivers, and support staff.

CareBrief helps you organize important care information in one place, then generate a clean PDF Care Brief that can be shared, saved, or printed.

What CareBrief Helps With

  • Save care recipient details and daily care instructions
  • Add important contacts
  • Keep notes and reminders
  • Create a printable Care Brief PDF
  • Include an optional Care Report page for caregivers to fill out
  • Import contacts from your device
  • Keep your information stored locally on your device

CareBrief is designed for family caregivers, parents, and anyone who needs to clearly hand off care information.

Privacy

CareBrief does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Information entered into the app is stored on your device.
