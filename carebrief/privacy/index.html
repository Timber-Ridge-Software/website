Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to CareBrief, developed by Timber Ridge Software.

Information Stored

CareBrief stores information you enter into the app, including care recipient details, contacts, Journal entries, reminders, and app settings.

This information is stored locally on your device.

Contacts Access

If you choose to import a contact, CareBrief requests access to your device's Contacts.

Contact information imported from your device is used to populate contact fields in CareBrief. It is not automatically uploaded or transmitted by CareBrief. Contact details may be included in PDF reports that you choose to export or share.

Notifications

If you choose to enable reminders, CareBrief requests permission to schedule local notifications on your device.

CareBrief uses Apple's local notification system and does not transmit reminder information to external services.

Data Sharing

CareBrief does not sell your personal information or automatically transmit care information to third parties. You control when information is exported or shared using your device's sharing features.

Data Deletion

You can delete information stored in CareBrief at any time using the Delete All App Data option in Settings. This removes your locally stored care recipient information, contacts, Journal entries, and reminders.

Deleting app data does not remove PDF documents you have previously saved, printed, or shared. Those copies must be managed or deleted separately wherever they are stored.

Deleting app data also does not cancel or remove ownership of a previously purchased Unlimited Exports unlock.

Analytics and Tracking

CareBrief does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party marketing services.

Exported Documents

CareBrief generates Care Briefs, Daily Journal Reports, and blank Care Report worksheets locally on your device. Depending on the document and options you select, exported PDFs may contain care recipient details, contacts, or Journal information. Once saved, printed, or shared, these documents are managed by the apps, services, or people you choose to share them with. Deleting data from CareBrief does not delete previously exported copies.

Children's Privacy

CareBrief is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date shown above.
